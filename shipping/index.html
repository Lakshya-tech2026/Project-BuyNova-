Everything you need to know about delivery.

Orders are processed after order confirmation. Processing times may vary depending on product availability, order volume and operational factors.

Delivery times depend on destination, shipping service, product availability and courier operations.

Tracking

When tracking information is available, customers may receive tracking details through the contact information provided with their order.

Delivery Delays

Delivery may occasionally be delayed because of weather, holidays, courier disruptions, incorrect address information or other circumstances outside our control.

Customers are responsible for providing accurate delivery information. Contact us as soon as possible if you notice an error.
