Returns & Refunds

Our return and refund information for BuyNova customers.

Return Eligibility

If you receive a damaged, defective, incorrect or significantly different product, contact customer support as soon as possible after delivery.

Return Requests

Contact support with your order number and a clear description of the issue. Photos may be requested to review the request.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed according to the payment method used for the order. The time required for a refund to appear may depend on the bank or payment provider.

Non-Returnable Items

Certain products may not be eligible for return because of their nature, condition, hygiene requirements or other applicable restrictions.
